## Ingredients

### Maple apples (makes 4 portions)

- 1 large (about 10 oz) | firm apple, such as honeycrisp, gala, or braeburn
- 1 tbsp | unsalted butter
- 3 tbsp | maple syrup
- 1 tsp | ground cinnamon
- 1/4 tsp | ground nutmeg
- 1/4 tsp | fine salt
- 1/2 tsp | vanilla extract

### Per bowl

- 1 cup | nonfat plain greek yogurt
- 1 scoop (about 1 oz) | vanilla protein powder

## Method

### Prep the apple

Peel and core the apple, then dice it into 1/2-inch pieces. Even sizing matters more than exact size — you want every piece tender at the same moment.

### Bloom the spices

Melt the butter in a skillet over medium heat. Add the cinnamon, nutmeg, and salt and stir for 20 seconds, until the cinnamon smells toasted rather than raw.

### Sweat it covered

Add the apple, stir to coat, and cover the pan. Cook 6 minutes, shaking once or twice, until the pieces have released enough juice to pool in the bottom.

> note: there's no added water here. The lid traps the apple's own moisture, which is enough — but it means an uncovered pan will scorch, so keep it on.

![Apples sweating under the lid](photos/apples-covered.jpg)

### Uncover and glaze

Take the lid off, add the maple syrup, and cook 6 to 8 minutes more, stirring every 30 seconds or so. The liquid should reduce to a syrup that coats the back of a spoon and the apple edges should brown.

> note: once the pan liquid is gone the syrup burns in under a minute. Pull it as soon as a paring knife slides through a piece with no resistance.

![Reduced to a glossy syrup](photos/apples-glazed.jpg)

### Finish off heat

Off the burner, stir in the vanilla. Let the pan sit 2 minutes — apples straight off the stove will thin the yogurt to soup.

### Whisk the yogurt base

For each bowl, whisk the protein powder into the yogurt in two additions. Work it until no dry streaks remain, about 45 seconds.

> note: dumping a full scoop into a cup of nonfat greek yogurt at once gives you chalky pockets that never disperse. Two additions is the difference between a base and a paste.

![The base, whisked smooth](photos/yogurt-whisked.jpg)

### Build

Spoon the yogurt base into a bowl. Pile roughly a quarter of the apple mixture in the center and scrape in the syrup clinging to it.

> note: refrigerate the leftover apples up to 4 days and reheat a portion for 30 seconds. Whisk each yogurt base fresh — protein powder stirred in the night before goes gluey.
