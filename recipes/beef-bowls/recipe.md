## Ingredients

### Beef
- 1.5 lb | ground beef, 96/4
- 1 tsp | neutral oil
- 3 cloves | garlic, freshly grated
- 1 tbsp | toasted sesame oil

### Sauce
- 1/3 cup | soy sauce
- 3 tbsp | brown sugar
- 0.5 tbsp | oyster sauce
- 1 tbsp | unseasoned rice vinegar
- 0.5 tsp | ground ginger
- 1 tsp | cornstarch

### To serve
- 1 cup | jasmine or short-grain rice, dry
- 4 | scallions, sliced
- 1 tbsp | toasted sesame seeds

## Method

### Start the rice
Cook the rice according to package directions. Start it first so it lands when the beef does.

### Mix the sauce
Whisk the soy sauce, brown sugar, oyster sauce, rice vinegar, ground ginger, and cornstarch until completely smooth — the cornstarch has to dissolve in the cold liquid or it will lump in the pan. Check that the vinegar bottle says unseasoned; the seasoned kind carries added sugar and salt and throws the balance off. Hold the sesame oil for the end.

### Brown the beef
Heat the neutral oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Spread the beef into an even layer and leave it alone for 3–4 minutes to build a brown crust, then break it into small pieces and finish cooking — 96/4 goes from done to dry quickly, so pull it a touch early; it finishes in the sauce.

![The beef in an even layer, crust forming](photos/crust.jpg)

### Bloom the garlic
Push the beef to one side of the pan and cook the grated garlic on the bare surface for 30 seconds, until fragrant. This takes the raw sharpness off — don't dump it straight into the liquid.

### Simmer the sauce
Pour the sauce over the beef and stir to combine. Simmer 2–3 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it thickens, turns glossy, and coats the meat.

![The glaze coating the beef](photos/glaze.jpg)

### Finish and serve
Off the heat, drizzle in the sesame oil and stir — simmering would cook off the nutty aroma it's there for. Serve over rice, topped with the scallions and sesame seeds; with lean beef and a sweet sauce they're doing real work, not garnish.
